## Reviewed, No Action

- `README.md` (32): `"A–Z"` uses an en dash as range notation — standard typography, not a placeholder. No action.
- Tool-suite job language (`index.html` resume builder / ATS scanner / cover letter sections, `src/app.js` ATS scanner and cover-letter identifiers, `README.md` tool-suite section): **intentional exception**. The tool suite is untouched by the agreed freelance-conditioning decision, so its wording matches its contents. Not a finding.
- Internal identifiers (`edit-job-btn`, `delete-job-btn`, `clJobSelect`, `rsJobDescriptionInput`, `populateClJobSelect`): not user-visible; renaming is churn with no user benefit. Not a finding.

## Passing by No-Change

- **R-25 (Color Contrast), R-32 (Keyboard Accessibility), R-03 (Mobile Touch Targets), R-09 (Badges), R-11 (Border Radius)**: `styles.css` is untouched since audit #001, and the v2.2.0/v2.2.1 changes were text-only — no new elements, classes, or colors. All pass.
- **R-37 (Design Direction)**: `DESIGN.md` exists with dials (ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1). Pass, subject to finding #4's wording fixes.
- **R-04 (Feature Icons)**: no new emojis added by v2.2.0/v2.2.1. Pre-existing ones are findings #5 and #7.
