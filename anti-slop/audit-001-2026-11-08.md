## Recommended Fixes

1. **Fix #1 (R-02)**: Replace all `"—"` placeholders with `"-"` or meaningful labels (e.g., `"None"`, `"Pending"`).
2. **Fix #2 (R-25)**: Update `--text-light` from `#525B6C` to `#94A3B8` (contrast ratio 6.9:1, passing WCAG AA) and replace hardcoded `#64748B` text.
3. **Fix #3 (R-32)**: Add a visible 2px focus ring (`border-color: var(--accent-orange); box-shadow: 0 0 0 2px var(--accent-orange-soft);`) on `.cl-document-textarea:focus`.
4. **Fix #4 (R-03)**: Increase tap target areas on mobile devices for action buttons via `min-height: 44px; min-width: 44px;` (or pseudo-element tap expansion) under `@media (max-width: 768px)`.
5. **Fix #5 (R-37)**: Create `DESIGN.md` capturing the Obsidian Dark design system, typography, and dials (`ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 1`).
6. **Fix #6 (R-04)**: Replace generic sparkle/lightning emojis with clean, purposeful SVG icons or plain semantic text.
7. **Fix #7 & #8 (R-09, R-11)**: Align `.badge` border-radius to `var(--radius-sm)` (4px) and ensure WCAG AA contrast.
